Common questions

Do I have to get on a call?

No. Most clients go from this page to checkout to onboarding without a call. Calls are only for custom or multi-location projects, or if you ask for one.

What happens after I start?

You'll get a welcome email with a short intake form and a link to pick your kickoff time. Most sites launch in 2 to 3 weeks after we receive your intake.

Is there a contract?

Standard plans are month-to-month. Cancel anytime with 14 days' notice. Founding-client pricing requires a 12-month term in exchange for the lower rate.

I already have a website. Do I need a new one?

Not always. If your current site is in good shape, we can take it over, fix what's slowing it down, and connect the systems in your plan. We'll tell you honestly after reviewing it.

Who handles computer and network support?

Tech support is delivered by our sister company, Axim Tech Solutions, which handles helpdesk, networking, and on-site IT work in the Phoenix area.
